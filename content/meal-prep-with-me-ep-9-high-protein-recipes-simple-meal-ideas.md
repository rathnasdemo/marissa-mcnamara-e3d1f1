## The teriyaki chicken recipe

*[Watch from 6:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BPE0zR1FEfY&t=384s)*

First things first, I'm going to go over the entire teriyaki chicken recipe with you. I literally just got this off of Tik Tok. It's called teriyaki chicken and rice bowls, and for some reason it's all in grams, so I'm just going to read it off in grams:

- 1,360 g of chicken breast
- 75 g of soy sauce
- 30 g of dark soy sauce
- 80 g of honey
- 60 g of mirin
- 30 g of ginger paste
- 15 g of garlic paste

We're going to cook on high for 4 to 5 hours. Then we add a corn starch slurry to help thicken the sauce, which is very important. We cook the rice separately and mix that in once the chicken is done. Then we make a little yum yum sauce, which is so good.

The one thing this recipe also calls for that I don't use is green onions. I don't care to garnish it. If you want to do it and make it look pretty, please do, but me, nah. We're just going to get in and out with this meal prep. I also forgot to mention that the recipe calls for 2 and 1/2 cups cooked rice, so if you want to get preparing that, go ahead. ## Cutting and seasoning the sweet potatoes

*[Watch from 11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BPE0zR1FEfY&t=670s)*

The rice is done. Now we cut the sweet potatoes up in small pieces. If you don't cut them small enough, they will be so big that they won't cook very well, and sweet potatoes already take a long time to cook. I set them at 400° and leave them in for like 30 minutes. I cut off all the weird little stringy bits and the edge. Don't cut a sweet potato the way I did. Mitch would lose his mind if he saw it, but not all of us are as strong as he is and can cut straight through a sweet potato.

Because all of my big mixing bowls are dirty, I mix the sweet potatoes in batches. All I do is add avocado oil. I eyeball it, and you can measure if you really want to, but maybe that was 2 tablespoons. Mix it up. Then I use very generic seasonings: black pepper, garlic, onion powder and salt. Mix it up again. I can definitely fit more in this, so I do it one more time, or probably three more times, with avocado oil and seasonings again. I eyeball quite literally everything.
