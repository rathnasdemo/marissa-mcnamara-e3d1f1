> **TL;DR:** This week Mitch and I meal prepped together for both of us, starting with a Costco haul and a few staples we already had. I made garlic soy ginger chicken on the Blackstone, sweet potatoes, egg white and cottage cheese muffins with chicken sausage, beef and avocado bowls, chicken and rice dishes, chia pudding and hard boiled eggs. My recipes are loose and easy to eyeball, and I do not prep every single meal I eat.

Good morning, you guys. I'm kind of excited for today because we're meal prepping. Typically I meal prep by myself during the week, because Mitch and I like to spend the weekends together. Today, though, Mitch and I are actually meal prepping together, and it's Sunday.

We do love to cook together, but it eats up so much of our day, and we'd rather be doing something else. Today Mitch said he was in. We both just got back from the gym, so we're finishing breakfast first. Mitch is making some eggs, and I just had a protein bar and might make a protein coffee.

## Breakfast and a cold brew taste test

*[Watch from 0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YL-kyaAVa8A&t=45s)*

My current obsession right now is cold brew. I got a brand called Bizzy, an organic espresso blend, unsweetened. I don't really know much about it other than it was on sale at Target, so I wanted that one. Alani also came out with 30 g protein shakes, and I believe they used to be 20. They have a cookies and cream flavor, which is my favorite, plus a frosted flurry one. I put it in the fridge this morning, and this is my first time ever trying a fruity cereal flavor, so we did a live taste test.

I will say the flavor is very good, but I do prefer a more chocolatey base for my coffee. It's not bad by any means, and I quite enjoy it, but a fruity coffee is just odd to me. I should have put two and two together. Also, code Marissa for Alani if you decide to shop. Not me going off on a tangent.

The plan from there was to finish breakfast, compile a little grocery list, and probably hit Costco, maybe Target and maybe more. Mitch also gave me a laugh. I had asked him to put an extra sausage in his dish for me, and he walked up with one baby egg, one baby sausage and a baby fork, saying he didn't want me to have any of his.

You guys know how lazy I am when it comes to making a salad. If I were to buy each individual ingredient, I would never eat it. Because I know that's how I am, I'll spend the extra $2 $3 or whatever on a salad kit for the easiness and the readiness of it. Yes, it costs more, but I'm not wasting money because it doesn't get thrown out. Some of you come at me for buying salad kits, and I get that they're more expensive, but that's better than spending $20 to make a salad and then never making it and throwing it away. We hate food waste.

Since Mitch and I are both prepping this week, there's much more food than usual.

## Rice and sweet potatoes

*[Watch from 4:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YL-kyaAVa8A&t=277s)*

The first thing I did was prep our rice. I think Mitch wants white rice this week. I get a lot of questions about my rice maker. We got it as a gift probably 2 3 years ago. It's one of the pricier ones and it's in my Amazon storefront, but I love it so much. If you're a family that makes a lot of rice, or has it weekly, it's the best thing ever. Also, don't forget to wash your rice.

Mitch then came up to tell me he was going to do a Facebook Marketplace sale. He was meeting a lady because he had just sold our old vacuum. Okay, Mitch.

For the sweet potatoes, you can skin them, but I prefer to leave the skin on, scrub them and get the dirt off. They were massive, and my cutting board looked way too small. I preheated the oven to 400°, and I got a lot of sweet potatoes cut up.

Then I seasoned them. I got this cutting board from hexclad, who sent it to me, but there's a crack in it, so anything a little juicy leaks to the bottom, which is annoying. I never do anything crazy with seasonings. We were out of onion and garlic, so I used some avocado oil, the tiniest bit of garlic, pepper and maybe Italian seasoning. I mixed it, because the bowl was so full, and added a little more oil. Then I spread them out on baking sheets.

Someone the other day commented that this girl obviously has no idea how to cook. You're right, I don't know what the hell I'm doing, but my best guess is that half of you don't know what you're doing either. We're all just trying to survive here. It's hard for me to watch cooking videos of professional chefs and think I'll do that. I need someone at the basic level with me, teaching me how to cook. I'm not trying to say you don't know how to cook. If you can cook, slay, I love that for you, and I'm so jealous. I just feel like it's easier to follow along with someone at the beginner stage too.

After that I set the pans aside while the oven finished preheating, and I washed a few dishes before the egg white sandwich sausage things, which are so freaking good.

## Garlic soy ginger chicken

*[Watch from 8:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YL-kyaAVa8A&t=513s)*

Next we prepped the chicken, because Mitch had the Blackstone ready. He also went out and got me a diet drink after his Facebook Marketplace deal. I'm trying to stop drinking so much stuff that's not water, but it's a treat.

Mitch has been making me a garlic soy ginger chicken every single night, and it has been phenomenal. It's so good that I said I need to tell all my fam online about this recipe, so you can have your chicken salads with me. We cut the breasts into the size pieces you like, such as strips.

A trick Mitch taught me: when you have a cutting board on granite or quartz, it slides everywhere. If you take a slightly damp paper towel and put it underneath, it helps prevent sliding and potentially cutting yourself. Things fly everywhere when you're cutting meat and the board goes askew, and you could cut your finger off. Not today, though.

The marinade was about:

- 1/4 cup of soy sauce
- Three big spoonfuls of garlic
- Probably 2 tablespoons of ginger
- A tablespoon of rice vinegar
- A tablespoon or two of honey

The amounts are kind of up to you, and you can't really mess it up. We mixed it and then took it to the grill. Mitch was prepping the Blackstone for me, and it's been quite the process because it's all rusty. Look how freaking good the chicken looks. Afterward we did the hard part, which was cleaning the grill.

## Egg white muffins

*[Watch from 12:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YL-kyaAVa8A&t=747s)*

We were kind of all over the place at this point. All the meat was cooked, but some of the sweet potato and the Normandy vegetables weren't done, so I couldn't plate anything yet. I got started on the little Egg McMuffin things instead. I had enough cottage cheese, hilariously, because Good Culture sent me PR, so I have a ton of mini cottage cheese things.

The plan was one egg per muffin, and then probably a cup of egg whites and one serving of cottage cheese, all blended. I ended up at about a cup to a cup and a half of egg whites, and the cottage cheese came to just under two servings. I use a cordless blender I absolutely love. I just press a button and it blends the entire thing. This is one of those recipes you can eyeball. These are going to be fluffy boys.

I put the mixture into each cup, then spinach. You can add whatever toppings you want, but I just do spinach and then chicken breakfast sausage links that I got from Costco, which came in a gigantic pack. I cut the sausage up, and I use one sausage per muffin.

I finished these and stuck them in the oven. The last time I made them I only had the oven at 200° and they took forever to cook. Today I'm doing 375 and just eyeballing them. I want to say it was 10 to 15 minutes, but I just watch them, because I kept changing the temperature when it was taking way too long.

## Beef, chicken and rice dishes

*[Watch from 15:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YL-kyaAVa8A&t=950s)*

Then I plated all of the beef dishes. Each one gets 200 g of sweet potato, 75 g of beef and an entire avocado.

I know a lot of you wouldn't put an avocado in your meal prep because you don't want it to turn brown. You can just add it when you're about to eat. I will say these are pretty well sealed, and the avocado doesn't really turn that much. You can also put lemon or lime juice on it. Which one is it? Okay, lime juice.

Mitch wants 300 g of rice in his dishes, so his will be chicken, rice and Normandy vegetables. For me I do 150 g, which I think is a cup of rice, so Mitch would be doing two cups. We had a debate about it, and in the end we decided on 250 g, because 300 seemed like the entire dish would be rice, and I wondered where the vegetables and the chicken were going to go. They get smashed in there.

Once both main dishes were done, the beef, avocado and sweet potato, and the chicken, Normandy vegetables and rice, I wrapped up the sausage biscuit muffin things. Last week I used a sourdough English muffin. This week Mitch wanted Bagel thins, so we're trying those, and I'm putting a piece of American cheese on too.

## Chia pudding and hard boiled eggs

*[Watch from 19:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YL-kyaAVa8A&t=1161s)*

With all the dishes done, the very last things were my chia seed pudding and the hard boiled eggs. We were so freaking close.

I always get tons of questions about my egg cooker. I got it from Target and it's from Dash. You poke little holes in your eggs, put water in and press start, and it makes perfect hard boiled eggs.

For the chia pudding, I'm going to be honest, I follow a very loose recipe. Mitch and I came to the conclusion that we do 2 tbsp of chia seed and half a cup of milk or dairy-free milk. Then I eyeball the fruit toppings, probably 30 to 40 g per cup.

## The recap

*[Watch from 20:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YL-kyaAVa8A&t=1259s)*

It was 7:00, I was so tired, Mitch was about to make me dinner and the kitchen was a mess. I was struggling to get through the end of that video. We still had a few more minutes on the hard boiled eggs, and then we were done.

I do not meal prep every single meal I eat. Here is how my week looks:

- **Breakfast:** my current hyper fixation, an egg white omelet with cottage cheese. I'll probably have the little sandwiches a few times a week, and Mitch and I both just grab them when we're on the go. Chia pudding in the morning.
- **Snacks:** my hard boiled eggs, and I typically have a protein bar during the day.
- **Lunch and dinner:** a ton of leftover chicken that I'll cut up and prepare, my Mediterranean salad with chicken and cottage cheese, and some of those beef dishes.

I don't know the macros for the chicken dishes you saw me prep for Mitch. When I do make those chicken dishes, because sometimes I have the exact same thing as him, I do 150 g of rice instead of 300 g.

When the eggs were ready, I put them in a little bowl of ice cooled water. I don't really know what this does, but Mitch taught me to do it, so I do it. Am I disgusting? I love the smell that hard boiled eggs give off, whether it's sulfur or what. It's kind of gross, but I freaking love it.
